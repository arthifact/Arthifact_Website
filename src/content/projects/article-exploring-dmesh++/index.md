## Overview

A brief exploration of **DMesh++**, a differentiable meshing framework connecting geometric optimization with gradient-based learning. Developed during the **MIT Summer Geometry Initiative (SGI 2025)** in collaboration with **Adobe Research**, this article examines how gradients flow through mesh connectivity and compares **Minimum-Ball** and **Weighted Delaunay Triangulation** methods for improved stability and accuracy.
